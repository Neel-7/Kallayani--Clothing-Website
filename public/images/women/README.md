# Women image set

All active Women photography is grouped in this directory. The current campaign, category, and product images were generated for their exact storefront placements and converted to WebP. Superseded photography is kept in `legacy/`.

| Placement | Files | Art direction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Home category card | `home-category.webp` | Contemporary dress and Jamdani saree against a teal doorway; square crop. |
| Home campaign slide | `home-campaign.webp` | Oxblood and ivory dress in a sunlit colonnade; wide responsive crop. |
| Mega menu | `mega-menu.webp` | Ink-blue wrap dress at a textile table with safe headroom; square crop. |
| Women page banner | `collection-hero.webp` | Two distinct generations in Jamdani and a modern handloom dress at an atelier; wide and phone crops. |

| Editorial archive in use | `editorial-*.webp` | Existing Bengal, South India, campaign, and wine-saree photographs used by editorial content. |

## Generation prompt set

- **Collection hero:** Two women of clearly different ages and faces in ivory Jamdani and indigo contemporary handloom clothing inside a Bengal textile atelier.
- **Home campaign:** One woman in an oxblood and ivory handwoven dress walking through a spacious sunlit colonnade.
- **Home category:** Two women in contemporary and traditional silhouettes against a weathered teal doorway, composed for a square card.
- **Mega menu:** An ink-blue handloom wrap dress at a textile studio table with generous headroom for square cropping.
- **Categories:** Nine different models or still-life concepts, each with its own age, styling, garment, setting, and palette.
- **Products:** Full-body studio photographs for Rakta, Nila, Sindoor, Rani, Shada, Neel formal dress, and Mati wide-leg pants. Distinct models, lifelike fabric, clean silhouettes, and no text or logos.
