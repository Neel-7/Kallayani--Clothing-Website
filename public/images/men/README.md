## Generation prompt set

- **Campaign banner:** Photorealistic editorial menswear in a Bengal textile atelier; two models of different ages, faces, and skin tones in indigo and ivory panjabis, centered for a narrow phone crop.
- **Home slide:** A single model in an ivory panjabi and indigo waistcoat walking through a sunlit Kolkata colonnade, framed wide with space for a lower title.
- **Home card:** A rust handwoven panjabi against a weathered indigo doorway, composed for a square crop.
- **Mega menu:** An olive handloom panjabi at a textile studio worktable, with the model's entire head safely inside a square crop.
- **New Arrivals:** A mineral-teal panjabi on a stone veranda.
- **Shirts:** A striped handloom cotton shirt in warm window light.
- **Pants:** Olive tailored trousers shown full length on a sunlit architectural terrace.
- **T-Shirts:** A brick-red cotton crewneck against a cobalt-blue studio wall.
- **Polo:** A dark green textured polo on a shaded modern veranda.
- **Accessories:** A cognac leather satchel, indigo scarf, belt, and watch arranged on warm stone.
- **Traditionals:** An indigo handwoven panjabi in a heritage courtyard.
- **Products:** Full-body models on seamless warm pale-gray backgrounds for Kaash, Indigo longline, Rong, Mati, and Megh. Distinct faces, lifelike skin and fabric, clear garment silhouettes, no text or logos.

The earlier Kurtas and Festive wear category photographs are archived in `legacy/`.
