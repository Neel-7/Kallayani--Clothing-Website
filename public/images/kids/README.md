# Kids image set

All active Kids photography is grouped in this directory. The imagery uses playful storytelling, outdoor movement, crafts, toys, and reading scenes to give Kids a distinct visual world from the Women and Men campaigns. Superseded photography is kept in `legacy/`.

| Placement | Files | Art direction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Home category card | `home-category.webp` | Three children in a cobalt and mustard cardboard playhouse; square crop. |
| Home campaign slide | `home-campaign.webp` | Two children running with a patchwork kite in an open field at sunset; wide responsive crop. |
| Mega menu | `mega-menu.webp` | Girl building a colorful wooden-block tower with safe headroom; square crop. |
| Kids page banner | `collection-hero.webp` | Four children carrying a handmade fabric fish through an outdoor art festival; wide and phone crops. |
| Kids categories | `subcategory-*.webp` | Seven narrative photographs for New Arrivals, Kids Partywear, Junior Girls, Junior Boys, Girls, Boys, and Toys & Books; 4:5 crops. |
| Kids products | `product-*.webp` | Seven full-body product photographs on warm pale studio backgrounds; 3:4 crops. |

## Generation prompt set

- **Collection hero:** A lively neighborhood art festival with four children, a giant handmade fabric fish, and paper streamers.
- **Home campaign:** Two children running through wild grass with a patchwork kite at golden hour.
- **Home category:** Children laughing through circular windows in a colorful cardboard playhouse.
- **Mega menu:** A focused, joyful child stacking handcrafted wooden blocks in a sunlit playroom.
- **Categories:** Paper crafts, a garden party, rooftop kite painting, a bicycle workshop, hopscotch, paper boats after rain, and a toy-and-book reading nook.
- **Products:** Full-body studio photographs for Ranga, Shada, Aalor, Khela, Chhoto, Tara, and Megh with distinct models and clearly readable garments.
